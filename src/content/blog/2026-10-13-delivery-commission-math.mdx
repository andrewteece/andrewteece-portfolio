Third-party delivery can introduce new guests to a restaurant, but commissions deserve a regular place in the numbers you review.

## Estimate the annual amount

Use this simple estimate:

**Monthly marketplace delivery sales × commission rate × 12 = estimated annual commissions**

For example, $10,000 in monthly delivery sales at an estimated 25% commission is $2,500 per month, or $30,000 per year. This is an illustration, not a quote or a statement of any platform's current fees. Your agreement, plan, market, and order mix can change the actual amount.

## Compare channels, not just percentages

Before changing your ordering mix, compare the full cost and value of each channel:

- What fees and promotions are attached to marketplace orders?
- How many customers are new to your restaurant, and how many are repeat guests?
- What does your POS or direct-ordering provider charge?
- Who handles delivery, guest support, refunds, and order issues?
- How will guests learn that direct ordering is available?

The right answer depends on your operation. Direct ordering can complement marketplace discovery rather than requiring you to leave every third-party platform.

## Start with your own numbers

Gather a recent month of delivery sales, fee statements, and order data. Then estimate the annual commission exposure and compare it with the cost of your current POS and any direct-ordering option.

An estimate is a useful starting point; confirm actual charges in your platform statements and provider agreements.
